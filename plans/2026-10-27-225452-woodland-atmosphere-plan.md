# Woodland atmosphere plan

Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-27-225452-woodland-atmosphere-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-27-225452-woodland-atmosphere-learnings.md`

## Goal

Enclose the environment test with a repeatable woodland, preserving the playable route, and add more dynamic local lighting.

## Approach

Use a seeded smooth value-noise layout with collidable near trees and a bounded low-cost distant tree layer. Add adjustable flickering torch lights along the approach and at the gate. Verify the walk to the gate, collision, light variation and both renderers at desktop and emulated touch sizes; compare the starting view.
