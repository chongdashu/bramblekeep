# Premise Plan

Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-27-170001-premise-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-27-170001-premise-learnings.md`

## Goal

Define a reviewable premise for a persistent multiplayer castle defense game.

## Approach

Draft the brief, label assumptions, and ask about the core action, camera, visit length and paid generation budget. Wait for confirmation before mockups or implementation. Maintain capture, journey and local commits at meaningful milestones.

## Verification

Check the brief against the user's words, validate record indexes and the journey entry, and review the commit.
