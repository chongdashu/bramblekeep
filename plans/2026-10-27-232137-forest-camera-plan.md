# Forest and camera plan

Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-27-232137-forest-camera-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-27-232137-forest-camera-learnings.md`

## Goal

Create a deeper wooded boundary behind camp and provide chase and free camera modes while possessing a character.

## Approach

Add irregular layers of shared textured trees and tune distance haze without obscuring the castle. Let chase follow the selected character and free camera stay independently positioned; preserve pan, orbit, zoom and released-character controls. Guard the selected actor from background-tree occlusion, verify the viewport and camera switching, and compare rear views.
