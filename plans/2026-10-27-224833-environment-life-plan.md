Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-27-224833-environment-life-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-27-224833-environment-life-learnings.md`

## Goal

Make the environment test a more useful live character and atmosphere study.

## Approach

Load all three rigged characters and let the user select, possess or release them. Add adjustable day/night blending, local camp lights, wind, drifting leaves and extra clouds alongside existing lighting, camera, shading and haze controls. Test on both renderer backends and desktop and emulated touch layouts.
