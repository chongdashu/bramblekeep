# Castle Sources Plan

Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-28-122336-castle-sources-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-28-122336-castle-sources-learnings.md`

## Goal

Separate the gate controls and give reinforcements coherent, protected sources in the castle and exterior.

## Approach

Move both levers outward, alternate spawn sources independently of the enemy slot, use existing solid castle exits and barracks, and add two solid hollow tree nests outside. Animate respawns emerging and keep the current spawn cap and safety distance.

## Verification

Check collisions, source rotation, builds, simulation tests and solo and two-client breaches.
