# Continuous stream plan

Linked prompts file: `prompts/2026-10-27-232949-continuous-stream-prompts.md`
Linked learnings file: `learnings/2026-10-27-232949-continuous-stream-learnings.md`

## Goal

Extend the stream continuously outside both sides of the playable map and use bank trees and rocks to reinforce the boundary.

## Approach

Extend the existing Blender-authored water, riverbed and banks to ±80 m while retaining the collision formula and bridge. Generate rock placement from a shared JSON layout used by the runtime collision and add collidable trees. Verify exported geometry and both renderers, then inspect the river from the bridge, looking both directions, and in portrait.
