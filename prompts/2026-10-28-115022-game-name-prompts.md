## Working Findings

Author: `Assistant`

```text
Working title: "Castle Adventure" (tagline "Camp-to-castle prototype").
It described the setting but not the play: nothing about co-op, the castle as
the target, or the garden creatures that defend it.

Essence found:
- Drop-in shared world; heroes (Knight, Spellcaster, Scout) travel camp,
  woods, river bridge, castle.
- Minions are living garden creatures: Bloom, Bramble, Mushroom, Stump.
- Two gate levers repair themselves unless kept under attack.
- Breach the gate, destroy the gold core in the keep.
- After victory the world starts a new round.
- Tone: warm, playful, storybook. Original art direction: "Garden of the
  Sleeping Keep".

Recommended: Bramblekeep. "Bramble" is the wild living-garden enemy;
"keep" is the castle destination. It suggests a castle that grows back (levers
repair, the world resets). Tagline proposed: "Storm the castle that grows back."
Possible lore hook: the gold core could be the seed that regrows the keep.

Alternatives offered:
- Briarhold: grander; no existing game found.
- The Sleeping Keep: mystery and lore first; does not signal attacking.
- Gatebreakers: says what the team does but is generic; several small
  "Gate Breaker" games already exist.

Search result: no existing game named Bramblekeep. The nearest is
"Bramble: The Mountain King" (2023), a dark Nordic horror adventure, a
different genre.

Open gap: the in-game castle is still clean stone with blue roofs; vines and
thorns on the walls and gate would make the name visible in play.
```
