# Gate Levers Learnings

Related records: `prompts/2026-10-28-095527-gate-levers-prompts.md` and `plans/2026-10-28-095527-gate-levers-plan.md`.

Each lever takes four hits and starts repairing nine seconds after its last hit. Both at zero open the gate for at least 20 seconds; hitting both postpones repair and can extend the opening. The short cinematic has a skip button and Enter shortcut, debris and a brief protection period; it does not pause the shared server. The core remains reachable from inside after resealing and an inside player can exit. The committed solo revision passed build, 304 tests and a real-input breach-to-core browser run. An isolated snapshot of the concurrently uncommitted multiplayer work passed 345 tests and a two-client lever synchronization, cinematic skip and timer check. Multiplayer integration files were already untracked/in progress and remain uncommitted; do not describe that snapshot as a clean committed release.

The levers and cinematic are prototype art. The screenshot is from the committed solo revision; no matched-camera concept comparison or phone visual review was completed.
