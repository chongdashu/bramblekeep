# Castle Breach Learnings

Related records: `prompts/2026-10-28-073720-castle-breach-prompts.md` and `plans/2026-10-28-073720-castle-breach-plan.md`.

Five patrol defeats open the gate while patrols replenish; six core hits clear the objective. Enemy types have two or three health. A swing checks overlap throughout its active interval, once per target, and C or the Hitboxes button shows volumes. Unit tests cover health, late entry into a hitbox, spawn limits, gate collision and core. A committed browser run defeated patrols, entered the courtyard and cleared the core, then opened the independent environment test.

The original generated keep is non-manifold: a Boolean cut left the door intact. Removing doorway faces made the passage visible, but leaves rough triangular edges. The front keep is hidden in Play while the player is inside so the camera can see the core. Enemies use procedural whole-model bob, sway, lunge and hit flash, not rigged animation clips. The courtyard and breach remain prototype art; no matched-camera concept comparison was available. Mobile input and portrait visual review remain unverified for this milestone.
