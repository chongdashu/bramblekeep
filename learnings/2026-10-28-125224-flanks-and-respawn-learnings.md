## Context

The earlier lever spacing did not produce separate fights, source entrances were easy to miss, and defeat returned players to camp instantly.

## What Happened

Levers are about 18 world metres apart, with a 14-second repair grace. Initial enemies patrol the level; replacements use castle side exits and exterior tree nests, with courtyard barracks joining after breach. Sources skip nearby players, and respawns produce a brief emergence cue. A player now falls, has a three-second countdown and returns to camp with temporary protection; other players see the downed pose. An isolated integrated snapshot built and passed 410 tests plus a real browser death and respawn. A two-client gate run passed, and a solo committed build completed the breach after its script was routed around a raised platform. The shared-world spawn and death changes remain in existing uncommitted multiplayer/courtyard work. Visual treatment has not been matched against a specific mockup.
